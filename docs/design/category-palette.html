Category colours: four shades each

One muted hue per category, spread around the colour wheel, each in the four roles Bill Rescue's Money scheme uses. Money keeps its current olive. The medium shade is darkened until labels read at 4.5:1 or better on the panel; white text on the dark shade is 8.8:1 or better in every case.

  • Three blues (Conversations, Travel & Events, Work & Meetings) are separated by hue and strength: cerulean, sky, indigo. They are the closest pair-set; worth seeing side by side on real pages.
  • Learning (ochre) sits near the gold "In a nutshell" panel, and Ideas (jade) near the teal related-guides panel. On those pages the side panels may need their own neutral tint.
  • Dark-mode versions are not shown; they follow the same rule as Money's (dark panel, light text shades).
