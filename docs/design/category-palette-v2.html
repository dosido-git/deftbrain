Category colours, v2

Fourteen calm colours, one per category, each with five shades for light mode and five for dark. Cool, clear greens and blues carry the "refreshing" side; warm tones stay soft. Text boxes stay white in light mode so it is always obvious where to type.

  • Every pair can be told apart. Checked by measured colour difference: label colours differ by at least 11.5 (light) and 8.6 (dark) on a scale where about 2 is just noticeable. Panels differ by at least 3.2.
  • Every label is readable: 4.6:1 or better on its panel, in both modes. Button text is 9.3:1 or better.
  • Labels and titles never match: the label shade is always clearly lighter than the title shade.
  • One change elsewhere: Learning (honey) is close to the gold "In a Nutshell" panel, and Ideas (lagoon) to the teal related-guides panel. With colour now carried by the category, both side panels would become plain white with a thin border.
