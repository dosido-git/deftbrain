Category colours, v3: pairs and two families

Each category now has a main colour and its own contrast colour. Half the categories are Deep (dark buttons, white text), half are Bright (soft pastel buttons, dark text), alternating around the colour wheel so neighbours differ in lightness as well as hue. Panels stay calm.

  • The contrast colour is used sparingly: a small mark beside the category name, the line under the title, and the border of "Today's only job". Never for large areas.
  • Every label, button and contrast text is readable in both modes: labels 4.6:1 or better, button text 6:1 or better, contrast text 4.6:1 or better.
  • Buttons alone are now distinct: the closest two (Sky and Cornflower, both Bright) still differ clearly, and they carry different contrast colours (raspberry and apricot).
  • Main colours are the v2 set, so nothing already checked gets lost.
