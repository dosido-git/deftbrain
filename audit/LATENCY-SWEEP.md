## Caveats

- Single measurements vary by roughly 20% run to run; re-measure before concluding a fix worked.
- Concurrency 4 inflates by about 8% against serial (50s vs 54s on `history-today`).
- Two entries are SSE streams (`contract-decoder/stream`, `sleep-architect/stream`).
  A stream that emits early keeps the connection alive, so total duration may not
  mean for them what it means elsewhere — verify before treating as failures.
