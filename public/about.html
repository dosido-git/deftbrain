About DeftBrain

A general chatbot is a blank page. When something specific is happening — your lease has a clause that smells wrong, your doctor said something you didn't understand, a project is so overwhelming you can't start it — a blank page is the last thing you need.

DeftBrain is more than 120 guided experiences, each built for one situation. Each one asks only for what that situation needs and gives you back something organized you can work with: the clause worth questioning, a plain-English explanation of what the doctor said, a first step small enough to start. No account needed. No blank chatboxes.

How can it be free?

DeftBrain is in its validation stage. The goal is to discover which tools people actually use. There is no catch waiting behind a signup wall because there is no signup. If enough tools prove useful to people, some version of a business may eventually follow. If we make that decision we will offer a valuable discount to early users.

How it works

Every tool is powered by a model from one of the best-known AI providers: Claude from Anthropic; ChatGPT from OpenAI; Google’s Gemini; or any of a handful of other models.

The models follow instructions we have painstakingly crafted and tested for each particular purpose. What you type is sent from our server to the relevant model. The model formulates the results and returns it to you. We do not store any of your input or results; not in a database, not in logs. Not anywhere. The details are in our privacy policy, which we encourage you to read because for once it’s short and contains good news.

What the Tools are not

Many tools deal with serious subjects—contracts, medical visits, mental health, personal safety, money. They are preparation tools, not professionals. LeaseTrapDetector helps you walk into a lawyer's office with better questions; it is not a lawyer. DoctorVisitTranslator helps you understand what you heard from your doctor. It is not a doctor.

Responses from AI models can sound certain yet be entirely wrong! Treat all output you receive as well-organized starting points. Verify everything!
